Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Each person can run up to 15 AI steps a day, which is enough for about three tailored CVs.

Will it make things up to get past the system?

No. It only rewrites what's already in your CV or what you tell it in your answers. A built-in check stops new jobs, degrees or dates from appearing.

Do companies reject CVs automatically for missing keywords?

Mostly not. Most recruiters read CVs themselves, but they search and skim fast. ResumePilot puts the skills the job asks for where a recruiter will see them, in your own true words.

Can I upload a CV in Hebrew or Arabic?

Yes. Upload it as it is, and choose English, Hebrew or Arabic for the final version.

What file do I get?

A Word file (.docx) you can edit, or plain text.
